Project Description

GT Movies Store is a full-stack movie e-commerce app: users browse a catalog, add movies to a cart, check out, leave and moderate reviews, and track past orders. Below is how each of the 21 user stories shows up in the actual pages. Click on any of the images below to enlarge.

Stories 1, 15

Homepage

The landing page introduces the store and its purpose (story 1), and is reachable from any desktop browser with no install required (story 15). The whole app is a standard server-rendered site.

Story 1

About page

A dedicated About explaining what the catalog offers and how users can rent/buy what they want.

Story 2

Sign up

New users register with a username, password, and password confirmation, creating the account needed to place orders, leave reviews, and report reviews.

Story 3

Login

Registered users log in with their credentials to place new orders, view past orders, and interact with the review system.

Stories 4, 5

Movie list & search

The Movies page lists every title in the catalog as a card (story 4), and the search bar at the top filters that list by title (story 5).

Stories 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13

Movie details & reviews

Users can add one or more copies of the movie to their cart (story 7). Users can also add a new review and modify/delete existing reviews that the user has uploaded (stories 8, 10, & 11). All reviews are under the movie description, aiding users in their purchasing decision (stories 12 & 13).

Story 21

Reporting reviews

On a review the user didn't write, the Edit/Delete controls are replaced with a "Report" button, so logged-in users may report inappropriate/offensive reviews and stop the review from bein displayed for all users.

Stories 6, 9

Shopping cart

The Cart page lists every movie a user has added, with quantity and running total (story 6), and a "Remove all movies from Cart" button clears it in one action when they change their mind about a whole order (story 9).

Story 14

Order history

Once a cart is purchased, it becomes an order on the My Orders page, so users can look back at what they've bought and what they spent.

Story 16

Responsive navigation

The same navbar (About, Movies, Cart, Orders, account controls) renders across every page and adapts to the viewport, so the layout holds up on different screen sizes rather than just desktop.

Stories 17, 18, 19, 20

Responsive navigation

The moviesstore admin panel allows administrators to manually modify, create, and delete all site models. This includes Users, Movies, Reviews, and Orders (stories 17, 18, 19, & 20).

Work Process

This project was built by following the book "Django 5 for the Impatient" by Dr. Daniel Correa and Mr. Greg Lim. It took about 5 hours of work, all done in Python 3.12 and HTML5. Upon any doubts, I avoided using AI tools to solve my problem, and instead used resources like GeeksforGeeks, Official Django documentation, and Stack Overflow (OG).

I learned a lot about the model-view-template server paradigm, and I am curious now to learn how Django itself works under the hood.
